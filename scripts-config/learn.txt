Keybinding Help 

Please note that the "Super" key is the "Windows" key on most keyboards, and the "Command" key on Apple keyboards.
 
Super+Enter: Open a terminal.
Super+D: Open dmenu to run commands.
Super+Shift+A: Open Nightshade Applications List to launch an app. Reads from .desktop files.
Super+Tab: Open our modified rofi launcher to filter open windows, launch applications, and run commands.
Super+Shift+Q: Close the focused window.
Super+Shift+Space: Float/unfloat the focused window.
Super+Number Keys: Switch between 10 workspaces (1-10).
Super+Shift+Number Keys: Move the focused window to a chosen workspace.
Super+R: Resize the active window with arrow keys.
Super+F: Fullscreen the active window (toggle).
Super+W: Switch to tabbed windowing.
Super+S: Switch to stacked windowing.
Super+E: Switch to tiled windowing.
Super+Shift+R: Restart the window manager in-place.
Super+Shift+Z: Lock your screen (uses wired-lock/swaylock).
Super+P: Take a screenshot with grimshot (copies to clipboard).
Super+Shift+N: Pop out recent notifications.
Super+Shift+F: Pop out file manager (nemo).
Super+C: Pop out a calendar window (gsimplecal).
Super+Shift+E: Log out of wiredWM.
Super+(left)click-drag: Re-organize tiled window or drag floating window.
Super+(right)click-drag: Manually resize the window.
