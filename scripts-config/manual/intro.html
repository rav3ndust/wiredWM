go back

navi is a minimal distro built upon Debian, and features our wired tiling window management setup.

wired is a heavily customized environment, bringing together a collection of FOSS tools to create a cohesive whole. It is keyboard-driven and tiling-first, placing your windows into a grid of tiles and arranging them automatically when new windows are open or closed. No more hunting and dragging windows around with the mouse!

wired follows our nightshadeNeon theme, and sports a Serial Experiments Lain aesthetic, giving it a retro-futuristic feel. We want wired to be comfortable and easy to use, while looking good doing it. A tiling window environment takes some getting used to, but once you've learned the basics and gotten your keybindings down, you'll wonder how you ever used computers any other way!

Computers are supposed to be fun. Remember when computers were fun? They still can be, thanks to the amazing world of GNU/Linux and open-source, everyone can escape the 'corporate' world of computers and make them feel truly personal again! We wanted to create a unique, bespoke environment that really speaks to what makes GNU/Linux awesome, and embrace it.

navi and the wired environment is built to be an easy-to-use, beautiful, minimal environment set up with our aesthetic and a slew of helpful apps and scripts to get you going right out of the box.

welcome to the wired!
